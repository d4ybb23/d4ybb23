> **Business premise:** Build a faceless, research-led business-documentary channel that explains the hidden economics of familiar companies, products, and systems. The business is designed around durable long-form viewing, original editorial work, and a deliberately human approval layer—not bulk automated uploads.

## Executive Decision

The selected working brand is **The Profit Map**. It gives the channel a clear promise—mapping the mechanisms behind familiar businesses—without trapping it in stock picks, news, generic “make money” content, or a single industry. The launch model is one original, 12–18-minute English-language episode per week after a three-episode pre-launch buffer. The main audience is business-curious adults who enjoy a good mystery but would prefer the mystery to contain a supply chain, membership fee, or strategically inconvenient spreadsheet.

This package translates the supplied blueprint into an operating system that can be used immediately. It includes a locked positioning decision, brand direction, a 40-topic scored editorial bank, production and publishing SOPs, launch-ready pilot packages, templates, analytics guidance, a 90-day execution calendar, and a revenue operation. The revenue operation creates a defined Evolve Human authority-film offer, commercial materials, verified affiliate/sponsorship readiness gates, pre-outreach target research, and a pitch deck—without converting independent editorial coverage into paid conclusions. It intentionally does **not** create a YouTube channel, purchase software, license media, accept commercial terms, generate narration, send outreach, or publish content. Those actions require account ownership, payment approval, explicit outreach authorization, or a final legal/brand review; every such dependency is labeled in `02_OPERATIONS/EXTERNAL_ACTIONS_REQUIRED.md` and `08_REVENUE_OPERATION/`.

## Autonomy Boundary

The work completed here is deliberately decisive: a name has been chosen, a production cadence has been set, first pilots have been prioritized, formats have been standardized, and the repository has been structured so a researcher, editor, or producer can operate without inventing the workflow from scratch. The following remaining actions are not delegated to automation because they create financial, legal, platform, or public-publishing obligations:

| External action | Why a human account owner must perform or approve it | Package prepared |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claiming a YouTube channel handle and creating the channel | Requires an owned Google/YouTube account and confirmation of availability. | Exact channel copy and setup checklist. |
| Buying stock, music, voice, editing, or design subscriptions | Creates a financial commitment and has changing license terms. | Vendor-selection criteria and asset-log templates. |
| Licensing or using third-party visuals | Rights depend on the exact asset, territory, license, and final context. | Rights gate and asset log. |
| Final render approval and public publishing | Determines a public statement on YouTube and final policy compliance. | Preflight and publishing SOPs. |

## Start Here: The First Nine Actions

1. Read `01_STRATEGY/CHANNEL_BRIEF.md` and treat the positioning statement as the editorial filter.
2. Verify the availability and trademark clearance of **The Profit Map** before creating the channel. Do not assume an unused handle equals a clear brand name.
3. Establish the production board with the fields in `05_TEMPLATES/EPISODE_MASTER_TEMPLATE.md`.
4. Produce the three pilots in the order identified in `04_PILOTS/` before publishing anything.
5. Create only the brand assets specified in `07_BRAND_ASSETS/BRAND_DIRECTION.md`; use no third-party logo, footage, or music without a reviewed record.
6. Use `08_REVENUE_OPERATION/01_MODEL/REVENUE_OPERATING_MODEL.md` to sell the client-owned authority-film cash bridge while the channel is being built; do not sell paid editorial conclusions.
7. Review and authorize any external outreach using `08_REVENUE_OPERATION/02_COMMERCIAL_ASSETS/OUTREACH_AND_DISCOVERY_TOOLKIT.md`; no outreach occurs without explicit approval immediately before sending.
8. Set up the channel using the copy in `02_OPERATIONS/CHANNEL_SETUP_CHECKLIST.md`, then publish Pilot 01 and apply the 48-hour observation protocol in `06_ANALYTICS/90_DAY_OPERATING_PLAN.md`.
9. Complete one retrospective per upload. Improve one weak link at a time rather than rebuilding the business because Tuesday was emotionally complicated.

## Evidence and Source Standard

All factual assertions used in pilots must be traceable to the relevant dossier and time-qualified in the narration and graphics. Primary company filings and official company disclosures are preferred for company-specific data; original explanatory graphics and licensed or owned footage are preferred over decorative borrowed clips. YouTube’s monetization policies require original and authentic content, and its review may consider the channel’s main theme, metadata, and the newest and most-viewed videos. [1]
