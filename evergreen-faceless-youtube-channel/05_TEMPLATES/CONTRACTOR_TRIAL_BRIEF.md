## 2. Creative Brief

The Profit Map makes research-led business documentaries about the hidden mechanisms behind familiar companies, products, and industries. The visual style is composed, high-contrast, and explanatory: deep navy/charcoal base, warm off-white type, one accent, purposeful motion, and original diagrams. The reference target is not “make it look expensive.” The target is “make the business mechanism easier to understand without turning it into a fluorescent algorithm carnival.”

## 4. Non-Negotiable Rules

1. **Do not use unlicensed music, footage, screenshots, or templates.** Candidate assets are not approved assets.
2. **Do not change facts, numbers, claims, or conclusions.** Flag a problem; do not quietly solve it with a new assertion.
3. **Do not use AI-generated imagery as a stand-in for a real company facility, person, product, or historical event unless expressly directed and labeled as an illustrative reconstruction.**
4. **Do not imitate another channel’s visual identity or recreate its exact thumbnail/sequence.**
5. **Do not publish, schedule, or upload the video.** All public actions belong to the channel principal.
6. **Do not treat generic stock as visual explanation.** At least one original visual mechanism should clarify the key claim in the assigned sequence.

## 5. Evaluation Rubric

| Criterion | Weight | What earns a pass |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Script comprehension | 25% | Visuals clarify the narrated causal chain rather than decorate it. |
| Asset discipline | 20% | Log is complete; no unapproved third-party material appears. |
| Visual storytelling | 20% | First seconds create a clean question and visual hierarchy. |
| Editorial precision | 15% | Numbers, dates, labels, and claims are faithfully represented. |
| Technical quality | 10% | Audio, captions, export, pacing, and project organization are professional. |
| Revision responsiveness | 10% | Feedback is applied accurately and without introducing new problems. |

**Minimum trial pass:** 80/100 with no rights or factual-integrity failure. A beautiful edit with an unlogged song is a failed trial, not a quirky exception.

## 6. Rights and Handoff Clause Prompt

Before engaging a contractor, confirm in a written agreement that the channel receives the agreed deliverables, editable project files, and necessary commercial usage rights; that all third-party assets are disclosed; and that any licenses requiring continued subscription are identified. Obtain legal review if the scope, jurisdiction, payment structure, or rights transfer is material.
