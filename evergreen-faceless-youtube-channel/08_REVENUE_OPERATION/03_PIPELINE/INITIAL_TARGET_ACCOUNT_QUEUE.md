# Initial Target-Account Queue — Authority Film Offer

**Reference date:** August 14, 2026
**Status:** Research queue only. No prospect has been contacted, qualified, pitched, or described as a client.
**Target geography:** Los Angeles and Southern California, selected to exploit local-market familiarity without pretending proximity is a strategy.

## Selection Logic

The Business Systems Authority Film is best suited to companies whose value comes from an operating system that is difficult to explain with ordinary product marketing. The initial queue favors business models with clear causal chains—logistics, industrial operations, enterprise workflow, agent operations, or complex vertical SaaS—where a documentary-style mechanism story could help sales, recruiting, category education, or partner understanding.

Y Combinator’s Los Angeles B2B directory describes 64 B2B software/services startups in the area as of August 2026, creating a focused starting universe for public prospect research. [1] Built In Los Angeles additionally identifies a broad local software-company ecosystem that includes operations-heavy businesses such as BuildOps and GrayMatter Robotics. [2]

## Pre-Outreach Research Protocol

Every Tier 1 company must pass this protocol before a personalized message is drafted.

| Step | Required evidence | Pass condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Primary-source review | Current company website, recent company announcement, founder post, or published case study. | One real, dated business signal is captured with URL. |
| 2. Story tension | A sentence that describes what the market may misunderstand about the company’s mechanism. | Tension is specific and non-flattering; it can be wrong and still be useful. |
| 3. Evidence availability | Public materials or a plausible subject-matter-expert source can substantiate the story. | There is enough material for a responsible first conversation. |
| 4. Buyer hypothesis | The asset could serve sales, recruiting, partnerships, category education, or product explanation. | A credible business use case exists beyond “they should make more content.” |
| 5. Outreach fit | A specific person, warm path, or public business contact exists. | Message can be personalized without scraping private data or guessing an email address. |

## Outreach Boundary

This queue is deliberately pre-outreach. Before any contact is sent, Arthur must approve the exact recipient group and the sending action. Each message will use a verified signal from the company’s own materials, will not claim prior work or results that do not exist, and will not confuse the Evolve Human client offer with independent editorial coverage on The Profit Map.
