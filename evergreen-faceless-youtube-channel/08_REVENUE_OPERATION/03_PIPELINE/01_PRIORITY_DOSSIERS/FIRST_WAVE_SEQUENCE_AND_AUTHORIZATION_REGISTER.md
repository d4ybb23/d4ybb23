## Send-Readiness Checklist

A target becomes eligible for outreach only when every item below is checked by the account owner or authorized sender.

| Check | Required evidence | Current status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Correct recipient | Named person and role verified from the company’s current site or LinkedIn. | Pending. |
| Valid contact path | Public business email, contact form, or warm introduction—not guessed personal data. | Pending. |
| Personalization signal | One dated, company-owned page or announcement captured in the dossier. | Complete for all three. |
| Message fit | Draft reviewed to ensure the exact angle is appropriate for the named recipient. | Pending recipient selection. |
| Commercial boundary | Draft does not imply a relationship, prior result, free spec work, or editorial coverage. | Complete; recheck before sending. |
| Send authorization | Explicit approval for the exact recipient(s) and message(s), immediately before action. | Pending. |

## The First-Message Rule

The initial message should offer a **20-minute mechanism conversation**, not a proposal attachment and not a free strategy workshop in a trench coat. If a prospect responds with interest, move to a diagnostic only after confirming the audience, evidence access, commercial use case, and scope boundary described in the corresponding dossier.

## Post-Reply Routing

| Reply type | Response action |
| --- | --- |
| “Interested / tell me more” | Send the one-page Authority Film offer and request a 20-minute discovery conversation. Do not quote an outcome guarantee. |
| “Can you send a proposal?” | Send a short scope for the Business System Diagnostic, including working investment, delivery window, exact deliverable, exclusions, and payment/approval conditions for review. |
| “We want to sponsor The Profit Map” | Use the sponsorship gate and disclosure library; do not convert the conversation into paid editorial coverage. |
| “Not now” | Thank the contact and record the timing/reason. Do not force a 14-part nurture sequence upon someone who has just said no. |
| No response | Follow the chosen cadence only after the account owner approves the follow-up approach. |
