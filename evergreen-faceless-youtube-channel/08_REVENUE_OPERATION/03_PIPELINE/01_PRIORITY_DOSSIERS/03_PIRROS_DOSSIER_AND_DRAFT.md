## 1. Why Pirros Is a High-Fit Prospect

Pirros has a precise and differentiated narrative: architecture and engineering firms do not lack design knowledge; they struggle to operationalize, find, reuse, vet, and improve it across projects, offices, and teams. Its public site calls Pirros an AI project hub for architects and engineers, combining detail/family search, quality-control workflows, standards management, and implementation support. [1]

The company has an active narrative moment: its news page says Pirros launched Mira, an AI agent for Revit, in May 2026, following a $15 million Series A announcement in December 2025. [2] It also publishes detailed customer evidence; its David Baker Architects case study describes a searchable, participatory detail-library workflow and reports internal customer results that can be validated directly with the client before they are used in a final asset. [3]

## 2. Story Hypothesis

> **Working title:** *The Detail Library Is Not a Folder: How Design Firms Turn Past Work into a Competitive Advantage*

The common premise is that every building is unique, so institutional design knowledge remains scattered in old models, naming conventions, senior memory, and disconnected libraries. The reversal: a detail is not just a drawing fragment. It is a reusable operating decision carrying quality, cost, risk, and workflow consequences. The film would show how search, standards, QA/QC, and project feedback form a learning loop that lets a firm reuse good work without freezing design judgment.
