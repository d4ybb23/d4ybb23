## 1. Why BuildOps Is a High-Fit Prospect

BuildOps’ core public story is not merely “AI for contractors.” It is the more specific, more defensible idea that commercial contractors need one operational record connecting service, projects, and financials. Its product site frames the platform as a connected system from field work through invoicing, while its 2026 materials describe an AI layer that uses work, crew, and job data to identify next actions. [1] [2]

The current narrative moment is also clear. BuildOps announced a new CTO in May 2026 as the company said it was expanding OpsAI and entering a next phase of AI-platform and enterprise-scale reliability; the same announcement states that more than 1,500 commercial specialty contractors use the platform. [3] This is a credible reason to discuss a mechanism-led authority asset now rather than present another generic “what is AI?” video.

## 2. Story Hypothesis

> **Working title:** *The Data Thread That Lets Commercial Contractors Run the Work Instead of Chase the Work*

The viewer begins with a familiar but messy scene: a contractor’s work is physically executed across crews, job sites, purchase orders, change orders, service histories, and invoices. The tension is not a lack of effort. It is that a single job becomes operationally invisible when the information trail breaks between field, office, finance, and customer. The film would show how a shared job record changes the decision loop—from dispatch and field notes to margin visibility and final billing—without turning the explanation into a product-tour infomercial.
