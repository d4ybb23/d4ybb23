# Outreach and Discovery Toolkit — Business Systems Authority Film

**Status:** Drafts are ready. No messages have been sent.
**Outreach boundary:** Sending any email, direct message, LinkedIn message, or form submission requires explicit approval immediately before the first external send.

## 1. The Outreach Principle

Do not lead with “we make videos.” That creates an instant commodity comparison with people who own a camera and a loyalty card at a coffee shop. Lead with the business problem: **a company’s real advantage is hard to understand, so prospects simplify it into the wrong category or postpone a decision.**

The invitation is a small strategic conversation, not a theatrical cold-pitch opera. The first goal is a qualified 20-minute discussion, not a proposal PDF attached to a stranger’s Tuesday.

## 2. Target Account Filters

| Signal | Why it matters | Disqualifier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Complex B2B or professional-service offer | A documentary explanation can clarify the mechanism behind value. | Commodity consumer product with no non-obvious system to explain. |
| Recent category creation, product shift, funding, expansion, acquisition, or change in go-to-market | Creates a natural reason to clarify the story. | No current business tension or credible narrative catalyst. |
| Evidence-rich operation | Makes a research-led asset possible. | Critical claims cannot be sourced or approved. |
| Founder/operator has a visible point of view | Speeds extractive interviews and produces a human story. | Only vague “we are the best” marketing language. |
| Sales cycle benefits from education | Asset can support sales, partnerships, recruiting, and category understanding. | Buyer needs a direct-response conversion ad tomorrow. |

## 3. Personalization Inputs

Before drafting an external message, identify one verified signal from the prospect’s own website, press release, talk, product page, or post. The message should name the signal and connect it to a legitimate story tension. Do not flatter reflexively; people can smell algorithmic compliments from a different county.

## 4. Cold Email Draft — Mechanism First

**Subject options**

- `The story underneath [Company]’s [product/category]`
- `A clearer way to explain [Company]’s advantage`
- `The [specific mechanism] most prospects probably miss`

**Body**

> Hi [First Name],
>
> I was looking at [verified signal] and kept coming back to a useful story problem: [one-sentence, evidence-based observation about the company’s mechanism].
>
> Most companies try to explain that with feature lists. We build research-led, documentary-style authority films that make the operating logic clear—what the system does, why it matters, and where the advantage actually comes from.
>
> I sketched a possible angle for [Company]: **[working story title or tension].**
>
> If that is directionally interesting, I can share the mechanism map I would test in a 20-minute conversation. No deck ambush, no generic video pitch.
>
> Best,
> Arthur

## 7. Discovery Call Structure

| Minute | Question | What it reveals |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 0–3 | “What are you trying to make more understandable right now?” | Primary job to be done. |
| 3–8 | “What does the market routinely misunderstand about how this works?” | Central tension and story gap. |
| 8–13 | “What evidence, customer language, or operating data makes the difference real?” | Source quality and claim discipline. |
| 13–16 | “Where would a credible documentary asset change the conversation: sales, recruiting, category education, partnerships, or something else?” | Distribution path and success definition. |
| 16–19 | “What would make this not worth doing?” | Qualification, risk, and decision boundary. |
| 19–20 | Recommend diagnostic, sprint, or no engagement. | Clear next step without pressure. |

## 8. Qualification Decision

| Condition | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Clear mechanism, evidence access, defined audience, and a credible use case | Offer the **Business System Diagnostic** or, when the decision is already clear, an **Authority Film Sprint**. |
| Strong business but unclear story or too many competing claims | Begin with the **Business System Diagnostic**. |
| Buyer needs guaranteed leads/views or asks for unsubstantiated claims | Decline or redirect; the offer is not suitable. |
| Buyer wants paid editorial coverage on The Profit Map | Decline the premise; offer a client-owned production asset instead. |
