# Disclosure and Integration Library — The Profit Map

**Use condition:** Select and adapt this language only after the relevant relationship has been approved, documented, and verified.
**Prohibited use:** Do not use this library to imply a relationship that does not exist, describe a tool not actually evaluated, or hide a material connection.

> The FTC says that a video endorsement disclosure should be in the video—not only in the description—and that viewers are more likely to notice disclosures made in both audio and visual form. [1]

## 1. Affiliate Link Disclosure

### Video — Audio and On-Screen, Before the Recommendation

> “A quick disclosure: the link below is an affiliate link. If you choose to buy through it, The Profit Map may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We only include tools relevant to the work shown here.”

**On-screen overlay:** `Affiliate link — The Profit Map may earn a commission.`

### Description — Immediately Above the Link

> **Affiliate disclosure:** The link below is an affiliate link. If you choose to purchase through it, The Profit Map may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. The recommendation is based on the use case described in this video.

`[Tool name] — [approved affiliate URL]`

## 2. Paid Sponsor Disclosure

### Video — Audio and On-Screen, Before the Sponsor Mention

> “This episode is sponsored by [Sponsor]. Sponsor support helps fund the research and production, but [Sponsor] did not choose this episode’s conclusion or control the analysis.”

**On-screen overlay:** `Paid sponsor: [Sponsor]`

### Description — First Screenful

> **Paid promotion:** This episode includes paid sponsorship from [Sponsor]. The sponsor did not control the editorial conclusion. The Profit Map’s analysis reflects the research and sources identified in the episode.

### YouTube Studio Requirement

Enable the platform’s paid-promotion disclosure for any qualifying paid product placement, endorsement, sponsorship, or other commercial relationship that may have influenced the content. YouTube defines these categories and displays a disclosure at the beginning of videos when creators identify paid promotion. [2]

## 3. Client-Work Separation Statement

Use this statement on relevant Evolve Human commercial materials—not in independent editorial unless disclosure is necessary to prevent confusion.

> “This client-owned authority film is produced by Evolve Human. It is not an editorial episode of The Profit Map and should not be read as an independent review or ranking.”

## 4. Editorial Independence Clause for Sponsor Agreements

> “Sponsor acknowledges that The Profit Map retains sole editorial control over its research, analysis, conclusions, title, thumbnail, and any discussion of companies or products other than factual verification of Sponsor-provided materials. Sponsor may request correction of demonstrably inaccurate factual statements about Sponsor, but may not require deletion of supported criticism or alteration of an editorial conclusion.”

**Legal note:** This is operational contract language, not legal advice. Have counsel review any final agreement, especially around ownership, indemnity, approval rights, category exclusivity, and claims.

## 5. Pre-Publish Disclosure Checklist

| Check | Owner | Pass condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relationship logged | Producer | Contract/program approval exists and is linked in the episode record. |
| Sponsor / affiliate statement is factual | Producer + relationship owner | Name, category, and compensation description are correct. |
| In-video disclosure | Editor | Audio and readable on-screen disclosure appear before or with the endorsement. |
| Description disclosure | Publisher | Clear disclosure is in the first screenful and immediately above relevant links. |
| Paid-promotion platform setting | Publisher | Set when required by YouTube’s policy. |
| Claims review | Research lead | Product claims are supported by actual experience or supplied substantiation. |
| Final sign-off | Account owner | Public upload approved after the above checks. |
