## Information Never to Store in This Repository

Do not commit passwords, recovery codes, API keys, credit-card data, tax forms, private contracts, unredacted invoices, unapproved voice-consent files, personal addresses, or account-owner identity documents. Store sensitive material in an approved password manager or restricted operations vault, then record only a non-sensitive reference in the episode folder.

## Contractor Boundary

A contractor can prepare a rough cut, source candidates, audio cleanup, captions, diagrams, or thumbnail variants. A contractor cannot silently decide that a claim is true, an asset is licensed, a sponsor is disclosed, or a video should become public. Final editorial, rights, and public-release accountability remains with the channel principal.
