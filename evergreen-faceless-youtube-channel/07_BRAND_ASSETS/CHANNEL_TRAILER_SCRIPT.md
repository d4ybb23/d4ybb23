# Channel Trailer Script — The Profit Map

**Runtime target:** 38–45 seconds  
**Purpose:** Orient first-time viewers, establish the documentary promise, and move them directly into the `Start Here` playlist.  
**Status:** Recording-ready script; use only after channel-name clearance and final visual review.

## Narration

> Every day, we use systems that quietly decide what things cost, how companies grow, and why some businesses become almost impossible to copy.
>
> Why does Costco charge you before you shop? How did a flat box change the furniture business? What does a restaurant chain actually control when most locations are independently operated?
>
> This is **The Profit Map**—research-led stories about the hidden business logic behind the things you use.
>
> From brands and retail to logistics, technology, and famous business mistakes, each episode follows the incentives, trade-offs, and systems underneath the obvious answer.
>
> Start with the first story. The map is already moving.

## Production Rules

The trailer must not make financial performance promises, claim to offer personal financial advice, show an unapproved company logo, or stretch beyond 45 seconds. The viewer should understand the channel’s question, subject range, and editorial tone in the first 20 seconds. No music, stock footage, or font may be used without an asset/license record.
