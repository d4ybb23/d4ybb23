## Usage Rules

1. **Do not add a company logo unless it is necessary to identify the editorial subject and the exact use has passed the asset/rights review.** The bases are intentionally generic to protect flexibility and avoid false brand representation.
2. **Do not place more than four words on a thumbnail.** Use the public title for the complete proposition.
3. **Do not introduce financial figures onto the image unless the figure is period-qualified and has an on-screen source in the video.** A thumbnail is not a mini annual report, however emotionally fulfilling that may sound.
4. **Retain the editable project/source file for any text overlay.** The PNG base should remain untouched so it can support packaging variants.
5. **Keep the avatar’s abstract mark intact.** It is a visual reference, not a claim that the channel owns a registered trademark.

## Final Acceptance Check

Before an asset is made public, review it at mobile thumbnail size, on a dark and light device theme, and in the platform’s crop preview. The pass condition is simple: the viewer can identify one visual idea immediately, the overlay text remains legible, and the visual promise matches the actual episode.
