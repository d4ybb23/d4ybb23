## Design Decision

No visual changes were required after review. The current composition intentionally preserves editorial restraint and the distinction between public-source observations, the proposed authority-film hypothesis, and the lack of a represented commercial relationship.

## Expansion Review — August 16, 2026

The BuildOps, Pirros, and Epsilon3 desktop routes were reviewed together. The shared Signal Room system held across the three pages: the left report rail, source ledger, route map, field-note stamp, independent-research boundary, and editorial hierarchy remained clear. The review identified one strategic refinement: the dossier system should remain coherent while each page’s route grammar and research artifacts reflect its specific operating mechanism.

The subsequent revision therefore strengthened the recurring four-node authorship mark, expanded the route-line motif through headers and section details, made the observed-evidence/proposed-lens/boundary taxonomy more distinct, and tailored route-map geometry to each briefing. Pirros now uses an ordered detail-library rhythm; Epsilon3 uses a procedure-grid rhythm; BuildOps retains the connected-flow rhythm.

The final desktop verification confirmed that the Pirros and Epsilon3 hero treatments now read as separate investigations while preserving the same Evolve Human authorship system. The Pirros route grammar feels ordered and archival; the Epsilon3 route grammar feels measured and procedural. Both routes retain the visible public-source and no-affiliation boundary at the point of entry.
